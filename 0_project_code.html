Explore Dolomites ⛰️

Dreaming of the Dolomites but worried about the cost or the logistics? You're in the right place!

This site is made for budget travelers and backpackers who want to explore the stunning Dolomites without renting a car.

We focus on the East & West Dolomites and show you how to get around using buses, trains, and cable cars, where to buy tickets, and how to reach the best hiking trails and viewpoints

Whether it's your first visit or you're just looking for the most affordable, easy way to explore, we'll help you plan a trip that's smooth, scenic, and unforgettable

Your adventure starts here — smart, affordable, and stress-free ✨
